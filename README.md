## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

* Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
* Provides logistics support.
* Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

* Better prices for farmers.
* Lower prices for consumers.
* Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
I propose developing a digital agricultural marketplace where farmers and Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs) can sell their products directly to consumers, retailers and bulk buyers.
Farmers can list their agricultural products, quantities and expected prices on the platform.
Consumers and buyers can compare prices and purchase fresh products directly from farmers.
The platform will help farmers receive better prices while allowing consumers to buy products at reasonable rates.
An AI-based system will help predict demand and suggest suitable selling opportunities.
Logistics support will help arrange transportation and timely delivery of agricultural products.
Innovation and Uniqueness: The platform combines direct farmer-to-buyer connections, AI-based demand forecasting and logistics support in one place, reducing unnecessary intermediaries.

## Technical Approach
The project will use modern technologies to create a simple and user-friendly digital marketplace.
Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript for developing the user interface.
Backend: Python for handling application logic and operations.
Database: MySQL for storing farmer details, product information, orders and transactions.
AI/ML: Python-based machine learning techniques for demand forecasting and predicting product requirements.
Logistics: Location-based services to support delivery planning and route optimization.
Implementation Process:
Develop separate registration and login options for farmers and buyers.
Allow farmers to upload product details, quantity and price.
Enable buyers to search, compare and order agricultural products.
Integrate AI to forecast product demand.
Provide logistics support to connect available transport services with buyers and farmers.
Develop an order-tracking feature to monitor deliveries.

## Feasibility and Viability
The proposed solution is practical because it can be developed using commonly available web technologies and existing digital payment and location services.
Technical Feasibility: The platform can be developed using Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript and MySQL.
Economic Feasibility: The project can initially be developed as a small-scale prototype with limited investment.
Operational Feasibility: Farmers, FPOs, retailers and consumers can access the platform through smartphones or computers.
Potential Challenges: Limited digital knowledge among farmers, transportation costs, product quality differences and internet connectivity may create difficulties.
Solutions to Challenges: We can provide a simple regional-language interface, FPO assistance, verified product listings and partnerships with local transport providers.
## Impact and Benefits

Helps consumers compare prices.
Reduces additional costs caused by multiple intermediaries.
Social and Economic Benefits:
